    How to read this page

    4W, 13W, 52W. Change over the last 4 weeks (about a month), 13 weeks (a quarter) and 52 weeks (a year).

    bp. A basis point, one hundredth of a percentage point. A yield moving from 4.71% to 4.96% has risen 25 bp.

    Net position. Futures contracts betting on a rise minus contracts betting on a fall, as of Tuesday, published Friday by the CFTC. Institutions are pension funds, mutual funds and insurers; hedge funds are the fast-moving speculators. Shown for context: the lab's test found it does not lead prices.

    Higher than X% of weeks. Where the latest reading sits among every earlier weekly reading, so 90% means near the top of its own history.

    4-week move bigger than X%. How this month's move compares with every earlier 4-week move in the same series. Above 90% is unusually large.
