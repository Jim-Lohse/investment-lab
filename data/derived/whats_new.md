How to read this. A flag is raised when a headline figure grew or shrank by 25% or more against the same month a year earlier, or when a country's share of what the United States bought moved by 5 points or more in a single month. That is arithmetic, not judgement: a flag says a number moved, not that the move matters. The daily summary decides which ones matter. Flags are early evidence that put things on the watch list; on their own they are not grounds for a decision.
