Macro Watch

Where interest rates, the dollar, gold, oil, stocks and global trade stand, and how they have moved over the last month, quarter and year. Built for tracking trends over weeks to months.

How to read this page

4w, 13w, 52w: change over the last 4 weeks (about a month), 13 weeks (a quarter) and 52 weeks (a year).

bp: basis point, one hundredth of a percentage point. A yield going from 4.71% to 4.96% is a rise of 25 bp.

Net position: futures contracts betting on a rise minus contracts betting on a fall, as of Tuesday, published Friday by the CFTC. Institutions are pension funds, mutual funds and insurers; hedge funds are the fast-moving speculators.

Higher than X% of weeks: where the latest reading sits among every weekly reading stored, so 90% means near the top of its own history.
